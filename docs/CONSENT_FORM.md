## About this session

We are testing a prototype wearable sensor that measures heart rate, blood
oxygen (SpO2) and fingertip temperature, so we can improve its software.
This is **not** a medical test, and you will not receive any health results.

The session takes about 35 minutes. You will wear the prototype sensor on
one finger and a standard clip-on pulse oximeter on a finger of the other
hand. You will sit, lift your finger off the sensor a few times, walk slowly
on the spot (or stand up and sit down), and do a short counting task.

## Risks

The sensors are non-invasive and use light and a small temperature probe.
There are no expected risks beyond mild discomfort from wearing a finger
clip. The prototype may beep; this is not a diagnosis. You can stop at any
time.
